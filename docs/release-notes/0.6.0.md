`gsfrip` runs on Windows 10 or later and requires no runtime DLLs. For macOS, the README explains how to build from source.

## Engine Software's driver

`gsfrip` rips the US release of The Santa Clause 3: The Escape Clause, 12 songs. Each song of the game's bank is a song, and it starts as the game starts it: the music volume rises from 0 to 210 of 256 over the song's first 53 frames. Song 5 is a jingle that plays once and ends. Song 6 starts at 210 of 256 at once and plays once: the game fades it out before it loops. Both get their length and a fade of 1 s. The other songs loop and get two passes and a fade of 7 s. The game's sound effects play samples without a sequence and aren't ripped.

Dragon Ball Z: Taiketsu and Ty the Tasmanian Tiger 2: Bush Rescue have other builds of the driver. `gsfrip` finds the driver in them and names the reason that it can't rip them.

The set was checked in mGBA 0.11. Each song plays the same trimmed and untrimmed. The set wasn't checked on the console itself.

## Other changes

- The drivers of Frontline Studios' two players share more code with Engine Software's. Their sets and their messages stay the same.

## Known limitations

- The Santa Clause 3: in a song's first 53 frames, the music volume is one step of 4/256 behind the game's. The fade-in starts a frame later than in the game. Songs 5 and 6 start at 210 of 256 at once and aren't affected.
- The limitations of 0.5.0 still apply.
